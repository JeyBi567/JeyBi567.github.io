Sweep

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version. Sweep runs entirely on your iPhone. It connects your phone directly to Telegram. There are no Sweep servers and no Sweep accounts, and we (the developer) never receive your messages, contacts, phone number or anything else from your Telegram account.

This policy explains what data the Sweep app ("Sweep", "we", "us") handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. Sweep is an independent app built on the Telegram API and is not affiliated with Telegram.

1. Your Telegram data

To tidy up your account, Sweep signs in to Telegram with your phone number and the login code Telegram sends you, using Telegram's official open-source library (TDLib). After you sign in, Sweep downloads information about your chats, channels, groups, bots, contacts, sessions and privacy settings, and parts of your message history, directly from Telegram's servers to your iPhone.

  • Processed on your device only. Cleanup suggestions, scores, badges, Wrapped, Friendship stats, Your words and On this day are all calculated on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Actions go to Telegram only when you confirm them. When you leave a channel, delete a chat, block a bot, remove a contact or change a setting, Sweep sends that request to Telegram on your behalf, and only after you review and confirm it.
  • Telegram's own policy applies to data stored on Telegram's servers. See the Telegram Privacy Policy.

2. Data stored on your iPhone

Sweep keeps the following in its private storage on your device, so it can work quickly and let you undo changes:

  • Telegram's local database and cached media, managed by TDLib.
  • A history of the actions you took in Sweep (including names and phone numbers needed to restore a removed contact), your keep list, badges and streaks.
  • Snapshots used by Contact insights and the Account monitor to notice changes, such as new logins or a contact's new number.
  • Your settings (language, appearance, reminders) and a small summary shown by the home-screen widget.

This data never leaves your device, except as part of an encrypted iPhone backup you control. Log out in Sweep's Settings to remove your Telegram session and local database, or delete the app to remove everything.

3. Notifications and background checks

If you turn them on, the monthly reminder and Account monitor alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. The Account monitor checks your Telegram sessions in the background using iOS background refresh. Sweep does not use push notification servers.

4. Purchases

Sweep Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Sweep checks your purchase on the device with Apple's StoreKit. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

If you don't have Premium, Sweep can show a rewarded video ad when you choose to watch one to unlock a tool once. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's software may collect information such as your IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Sweep does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps, so your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Sweep asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are loaded. The ad software is not started for Premium users. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't run servers that receive your data, and we don't have a Sweep account system.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • Sharing cards (like your clutter score or Wrapped) only leave your phone when you share them yourself.

7. Children

Sweep is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required to use Telegram where you live.

8. Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data in the app (Log out, or delete the app) and in Telegram (Settings → Privacy and Security). For data collected by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls. If you have a question or a request, contact us and we'll help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
