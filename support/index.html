Common questions

Is it safe to sign in with my Telegram account?

Sweep signs in with Telegram's official login code and Telegram's open-source library, the same way other Telegram apps do. Everything runs on your iPhone. There are no Sweep servers, and we never see your chats. Sweep also appears in Telegram → Settings → Devices, and you can end its session there at any time.

Will Sweep delete anything without asking?

No. Every tool shows you a list first, and nothing changes until you confirm. Swipe left on any item and tap Keep so it's never suggested again.

Can I undo a cleanup?

Most actions can be undone from Recently swept: rejoin public channels and groups, re-add contacts, unblock bots and re-install sticker packs. Some actions are permanent and Sweep says so before you confirm, for example erasing your messages for everyone, deleting a chat for yourself, or leaving a private group (you'll need a new invite).

Why is Sweep going slowly?

Telegram limits how many actions an account can do in a short time. Sweep paces bulk actions and waits when Telegram asks it to, so your account isn't flagged.

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone?

Open Sweep Premium in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language, or the language button on the sign-in screen. Sweep speaks 15 languages.

How do I remove my data?

Settings → Log out removes your Telegram session and the local database from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Sweep made by Telegram?

No. Sweep is an independent app built on the Telegram API and isn't affiliated with Telegram.
